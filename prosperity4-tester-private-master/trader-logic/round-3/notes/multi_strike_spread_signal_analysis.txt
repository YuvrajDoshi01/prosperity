================================================================================
MULTI-STRIKE SPREAD SIGNAL ANALYSIS
Can the VEV_4000 spread=22 signal (vev3.py) generalize to other voucher strikes?
================================================================================
Date: 2026-04-26
Data: Round 3, days 0/1/2, 10k ticks each (30k total)
Method: Forward VFE returns after spread-widen events per strike
Source: voucher_spread_signal_analysis.py → voucher_spread_signal_results.txt

================================================================================
ANSWER: YES — the signal is UNIVERSAL, not VEV_4000-specific.
================================================================================

Every voucher strike from VEV_4000 through VEV_5300 shows statistically
significant negative VFE forward returns after spread-widen events.
20 of 60 hypotheses survive Bonferroni correction at 1% FWER.

The mechanism: when an option's bid-ask spread widens beyond its normal
(mode) value, it indicates the MM bot is repricing — which happens at
VFE local peaks. Shorting VFE at these moments captures mean reversion.

2. KEY DISCOVERY: VEV_5200 IS BETTER THAN VEV_4000
================================================================================

Direct comparison (consec>=1):

  Metric              VEV_4000    VEV_5200    Delta
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Mean return @200    -5.33       -7.93       +49% better
  Hit rate @200       67.3%       76.0%       +8.7pp
  Mean return @100    -2.70       -4.54       +68% better
  Hit rate @100       61.4%       70.6%       +9.2pp
  Mean return @50     -1.47       -2.57       +75% better
  Hit rate @50        59.0%       65.0%       +6.0pp
  Peak-bias           62.8%       66.4%       +3.6pp
  Events/day          ~1,049      ~393        fewer but cleaner

VEV_5200 is near-ATM (strike=5200, VFE~5000-5200, delta~0.4-0.5). Its spread
rarely widens (only 4.4% of day2 ticks), so when it DOES widen, it's a
stronger regime signal.

WHY is VEV_5200 better?
- Near-ATM options have highest gamma → most sensitive to directional moves
- The MM bot reprices ATM options more aggressively at VFE peaks
- Spread=3→4 is a 33% increase (vs 21→22 = 4.8% for VEV_4000)
- Proportional spread change matters more than absolute

================================================================================
3. MIRROR SIGNAL: SPREAD NARROWING = LONG
================================================================================

The symmetric pattern exists. When spreads narrow BELOW mode, VFE is at a
local trough and will rise:

  Strike     Narrow value    Mean@50    t-stat    Interpretation
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  VEV_4000   spread=20       +1.84      +15.3     BUY signal
  VEV_4500   spread=15       +1.75      +16.4     BUY signal
  VEV_5000   spread=5        +2.19      +15.3     BUY signal
  VEV_5200   spread=2        +1.41      +13.3     BUY signal
  VEV_5300   spread=1        +1.69      +7.2      BUY signal

This could replace the bottom-flip logic in vev3.py with a more robust,
multi-strike confirmation. Currently vev3 uses VFE price quantile for
the bottom flip — spread narrowing across multiple strikes would be a
stronger signal.

================================================================================
4. CROSS-STRIKE CORRELATION: LOW → ENSEMBLE IS VALUABLE
================================================================================

Jaccard similarity between widen-event timestamps:

  VEV_4000 vs VEV_4500: 0.03 (97% unique — different regime)
  VEV_4000 vs VEV_5200: 0.11 (89% unique)
  VEV_4500 vs VEV_5200: 0.11 (89% unique)
  VEV_5100 vs VEV_5400: 0.26 (highest, but still 74% unique)

Low correlation means:
1. Signals are NOT redundant — different strikes detect different peaks
2. A multi-strike ensemble captures more events
3. Simultaneous widening across K>=2 strikes → stronger confirmation

Practical ensemble approach:
  IF widen on VEV_4000 AND widen on VEV_5200 → strongest short signal
  IF widen on VEV_4500 AND widen on VEV_5000 → secondary confirmation
  Require 2+ Tier 1/2 strikes widening → filter out false positives

================================================================================
5. PRACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

A. UPGRADE vev3.py SIGNAL (drop-in replacement):

  Current: VEV_4000 spread==22, consec>=3
  Proposed: VEV_5200 spread==4, consec>=1 (or consec>=2 for safety)

  Expected improvement: +49% per-event return, +9pp hit rate.
  Caveat: 63% fewer events per day (393 vs 1049).

B. MULTI-STRIKE ENSEMBLE (higher capacity):

  Count how many Tier 1+2 strikes are widening simultaneously:
    score = sum(1 for K in [4000, 4500, 5000, 5200] if spread[K] > mode[K])

  Entry: score >= 2 → short VFE to -200
  Exit: score == 0 AND spread narrowing on 2+ strikes → flip long

  This captures more events (union of 4 strikes) with higher precision
  (confirmation reduces false positives).

C. REPLACE BOTTOM-FLIP WITH SPREAD NARROWING:

  Current vev3: exit short when VFE <= 8th percentile of 500-tick window
  Proposed: exit short when spread NARROWS on 2+ strikes simultaneously

  Spread narrowing is a leading indicator (MM repricing), while price
  quantile is a lagging indicator (requires VFE to already be low).

  Expect: earlier entry into flip → capture more of the recovery move

D. CAPACITY CONSTRAINT:

  VFE limit = 200 contracts. Can only hold ONE directional position.
  Multi-strike signals don't multiply positions — they improve TIMING.
  The binding constraint is always: how many directional round-trips
  per day, and what's the average profit per trip.

  vev3 current: ~2-4 round trips/day, ~$5-10k/trip → $20-30k/day
  With better entry timing: same trips but +30-50% per trip

================================================================================
6. SIGNALS TO AVOID
================================================================================

VEV_5400: mode=1, widen fires 38% of ticks. Signal-to-noise ratio is
essentially zero (hit rate 50.9%). The spread toggles between 1 and 2
as a structural feature of the book, not a regime indicator.

VEV_5500: Same issue, slightly less noisy (widen 15% of ticks) but still
hit rate only 51.8%. Not worth the implementation complexity.

VEV_6000/6500: Constant spread=1, zero information content.

VEV_4500 as standalone: While the signal quality matches VEV_4000, the
VEV_4500 market is essentially dead (1 trade in 30k ticks). The spread
data is from the MM bot quotes, which are reliable, but there's no
market validation of the book quality. Use as CONFIRMATION only.
