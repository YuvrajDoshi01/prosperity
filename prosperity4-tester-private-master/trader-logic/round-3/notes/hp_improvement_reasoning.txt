HP IMPROVEMENT REASONING — "Profiting from the drop more efficiently"
======================================================================
Date: 2026-04-26
Status: Analysis only, no code changes

CONTEXT
-------
Current best: v22 (shipped as v11 variant). HP generates $10-53k/day depending
on regime. The user observes HP "goes down from its local high" and wants to
capture that drop more efficiently.

Current v22 HP logic:
  1. Wait for spread==17 AND mid > 10010 → SHORT to -200
  2. Wait for spread==7 AND mid <= 8th-percentile(500-tick window) → COVER + FLIP to +200
  3. HOLD +200 until mid >= 10020 or 1500-tick timeout → FLATTEN
  4. Otherwise → passive MM (edge-beta calibrated, QUOTE_SIZE=25)

========================================================================
PART 1: WHAT THE DATA ACTUALLY SAYS ABOUT "DROPS FROM HIGHS"
========================================================================

HP is a bounded OU process centered at ~9990 with std ~31. The AC(1) on returns
is -0.13 (mild mean reversion per tick). Spread=16 dominates 92-93% of ticks.

SIGNAL RANKING (forward return at t+200, all 3 days):

  Rank  Signal                          Mean     N/day  Win%  Cross-day?
  ----  -----                           ----     -----  ----  ----------
  1     spread==17 & mid>10010          -18.01   ~117   79.5  YES
  2     spread==17 (any mid)            -17.40   ~128   77.0  YES
  3     mid > ma200 + 20               -10.78   ~780   66.9  YES
  4     sp>=15 & mid>ma200+10 & roc>0   -7.50   ~1692  61.1  YES
  5     mid > ma200 + 10                -7.27   ~2282  62.0  YES
  6     mid < ma500 - 15 (LONG)        +9.50    ~1900  67.0  YES
  7     mid < ma200 - 20 (LONG)        +12.10   ~760   67.7  YES

The spread=17 signal is overwhelmingly the best per-event, but it fires only
~130 times/day. The mid>ma200+20 signal fires 6x more often with 2/3 the edge.

========================================================================
PART 2: WHERE THE CURRENT STRATEGY LEAVES MONEY ON THE TABLE
========================================================================

Problem 1: BINARY ON/OFF — Only one short signal (spread==17)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Between S17 events (which are 1.3% of ticks), the strategy sits in passive MM.
During those 9,870 ticks/day, mid>ma200+20 fires ~780 times with -10.8 expected
return at t+200, 67% win rate. That's 780 * 10.8 * (some fill fraction) of
unrealized edge. Even at 30% capture with half-size (100 units):
  780 * 0.30 * 10.8 * (100/200) = ~$1,263/day of untapped alpha.

The strategy currently earns $0 directional PnL during these 9,870 "dead" ticks
(passive MM earns spread capture but zero directional).

Problem 2: FLIP EXIT IS CRUDE
------------------------------
After S7 cover + flip to +200, v22 holds until mid >= 10020 or 1500-tick timeout.
Analysis from missing_alpha/02_flip_optimal_exit.py shows:
  - Optimal mid-target is 10010-10015, not 10020
  - 10020 works best on day 2 but leaves PnL on table on days 0/1 where mid
    often doesn't reach 10020, triggering the 1500-tick timeout instead
  - A trailing stop (v26 approach: TRAIL_DELTA=15 from peak) exits earlier
    when the rebound stalls, capturing 60-80% of the move vs 40-60% with fixed

The timeout exit at 1500 ticks is particularly wasteful. If mid reaches 10012
at tick 800 then drifts sideways, you hold for 700 more ticks earning nothing
while a trailing stop would have exited at 10012-15=9997... wait, that's wrong.
The trailing stop tracks the peak and exits when mid drops TRAIL_DELTA below it.
So if peak=10012, exit at 10012-15=9997. That's actually worse than holding.

Better approach: EXIT when mid >= 10010 OR (peak_since_flip > 10005 AND
mid < peak_since_flip - 8). This captures the "mid reached a decent level but
is now rolling over" case without the pathological timeout.

Problem 3: PASSIVE MM BLEEDS IN TIGHT SPREADS
----------------------------------------------
Analysis from hp_deadzone_analysis.py and hp_inv_aware_mm.py shows:
  - Wide spread (>=9) passive MM: profitable, ~3 ticks/fill captured
  - Tight spread (7-8): adverse selection dominates, losing money
  - Inventory suppression at |pos|>80 partially fixes this

Current v22 posts passive quotes at ALL spreads. Suppressing quotes when
spread <= 8 removes the bleed source. Cost: ~5-10% fewer fills in dead zones.
Benefit: eliminates the adverse selection drag.

Problem 4: NO INDEPENDENT LONG ENTRY
--------------------------------------
The only way v22 goes long is via the S7 cover+flip (which requires a prior
S17 short). Independent long entries at mid < ma200 - 20 have:
  t+200: +12.1, 67.7% win rate, ~760 events/day

This is comparable in magnitude to the short signals. Currently 100% untapped.
A simple "if mid < ma200 - 20 AND position >= 0 AND no S17 active → BUY to
+100 (half-size, conservative)" would add structural alpha.

Problem 5: SIZING IS ALWAYS MAX
---------------------------------
v22 always goes to ±200 on S17 entry and ±200 on FLIP. The advisor's principle
applies here too: scale exposure to signal strength.

  - spread==17 & mid > 10030 (99th percentile of S17 mids): go full -200
  - spread==17 & mid in [10010, 10020]: go -150
  - mid > ma200+20 (no S17): go -100
  - mid > ma200+10 (weakest): go -50

This reduces drawdown on false signals while maintaining full exposure on the
highest-conviction entries.

========================================================================
PART 3: WHAT WOULD A "MORE EFFICIENT" HP STRATEGY LOOK LIKE?
========================================================================

Layer architecture (non-overlapping, priority-ordered):

  LAYER 0 (highest priority): S17 GIGA SHORT
    Trigger: spread==17 AND mid > 10010
    Action:  SHORT to -200 aggressively (hit bid)
    Exit:    S7 bottom (existing) or adaptive trailing stop
    Edge:    -18.01 at t+200, 79.5% win rate
    Status:  ALREADY IMPLEMENTED in v22

  LAYER 1: TREND-REVERSION SHORT
    Trigger: mid > ma200 + 20 AND no S17 active AND position > -100
    Action:  SHORT to max(-100, -(mid - ma200) * 5) [scaled]
    Exit:    mid < ma200 + 5 (revert to mean vicinity)
    Edge:    -10.78 at t+200, 66.9% win rate
    Status:  NOT IMPLEMENTED — biggest marginal gain

  LAYER 2: MEAN-REVERSION LONG
    Trigger: mid < ma200 - 20 AND no S17/S7 active AND position < +100
    Action:  BUY to min(+100, (ma200 - mid) * 5) [scaled]
    Exit:    mid > ma200 - 5
    Edge:    +12.10 at t+200, 67.7% win rate
    Status:  NOT IMPLEMENTED — symmetric to Layer 1

  LAYER 3: FLIP HOLD (improved exit)
    Trigger: FLIP complete (position == +200)
    Exit:    mid >= 10010 (lowered from 10020)
             OR (peak_since_flip >= 10005 AND mid < peak - 8)
             OR timeout 1000 ticks (reduced from 1500)
    Status:  PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED (v22 has 10020/1500)

  LAYER 4: PASSIVE MM (filtered)
    Trigger: no directional signal active AND spread >= 9
    Action:  quote bid/ask at mid ± skew, QUOTE_SIZE=25
    Exit:    continuous
    Filter:  SUPPRESS when spread <= 8 (avoid adverse selection)
    Status:  PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED (v22 doesn't filter by spread)

========================================================================
PART 5: WHAT WON'T WORK (ALREADY TESTED)
========================================================================

1. Aggressive take at S17 (TAKE_OFFSET > 0): -$80k 3-day from adverse selection
   on MM bot quotes. Never cross at worse-than-best.

2. S15 GIGA LONG (mirror of S17 SHORT): -$8.9k 3-day. Spread=15 is 2.2-2.9% of
   ticks but NOT a reversal signal — it's just slightly below normal=16.

3. Taker flow prediction: AC < 2 on all 3 days. Not actionable.

4. Volatility-based timing: realized vol correlation with spread = -0.03. Useless.

5. OBI alpha: beta=0.30 but spread=20 >> signal. Always spread-dominated.

6. IV smile z-score for timing HP: half-life 1-30 ticks, not 100-200. Too fast.

========================================================================
PART 6: RISKS AND CAVEATS
========================================================================

1. All signals are tested on the 3 CSV days provided. The live round may have
   different microstructure (different bot parameters, different TTE, etc.).

2. The ma200 signals (Layers 1 and 2) require a 200-tick warmup. First 200 ticks
   of day 2 (the website test window) will miss early signals.

3. Position limit interactions: if Layer 1 puts us at -100 and then S17 fires,
   we need headroom to go to -200. Must enforce priority ordering.

4. The BT overpredicts fills by ~30-56% vs website (from R1 calibration). Edges
   will be smaller live. The 67% win rate on Layer 1 may drop to 60% live.

5. Adding more layers increases code complexity and traderData size. The 50KB
   traderData cap is a hard constraint. Each rolling buffer costs bytes.

========================================================================
PART 7: BOTTOM LINE
========================================================================

The biggest single improvement is LAYER 1 (trend-reversion shorts when
mid > ma200 + 20). It fires 780 times/day with -10.8 expected return, is
stable across all 3 days, requires no new data structures (just a rolling
mean you're already computing), and slots cleanly below the S17 priority.

The second biggest is LAYER 2 (mean-reversion longs from below). The long side
is actually MORE consistent cross-day than the short side (the long mean is
+12.1 vs short -10.8, and the cross-day variance is lower).

Together these two layers could add $3-7k/day to the existing $20-30k HP
baseline, all from structural signals that work on every CSV day.

The FLIP exit improvement (Layer 3) is lower risk, lower reward: ~$1-2k/day
from tightening the exit target from 10020 to 10010 and reducing timeout from
1500 to 1000.

None of these require hardcoded trajectories or memorized data. All are
structural signals derived from the OU-process microstructure of HP.
