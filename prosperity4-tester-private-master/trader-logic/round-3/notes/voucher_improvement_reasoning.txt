PART 3: WHERE THE CURRENT STRATEGY LOSES EDGE
========================================================================

Current v22 voucher layers and their problems:

LAYER: Intrinsic arb (buy below intrinsic - 2)
  Status: Fine but rarely fires. VEV_5000 intrinsic ~253, market mid ~256.
  Only triggers on extreme dislocations. Adds ~$0-286/day.

LAYER: BS taking (buy if ask < BS_fair - 10, sell if bid > BS_fair + 10)
  Problem: BS_EDGE=10 is EXTREMELY wide. VEV_5300 mid=47, so fair-10=37 —
  this only fires if the ask drops to 37, which basically never happens for
  OTM strikes. On day 2 1k-tick, BS taking earns $0 on 5300/5400/5500.
  On 10k day 2, it earns $762 on 5300, $546 on 5400 — tiny.
  The BS_EDGE was calibrated for safety but it's TOO safe for OTM.

LAYER: Passive MM on tradeable strikes (bid at bb+1, ask at ba-1, size 20)
  Problem: On 5400/5500 where spread=1, bb+1 = ba = ask. So our "bid" is
  at the ask, which immediately crosses and we buy at the ask. Not passive
  at all — we're aggressive-taking at the ask, which is the WRONG direction
  for OTM calls with 100% seller flow.
  On 5000 (spread=6), this works well. On 5300 (spread=2), marginal.

LAYER: OTM passive bids (5300/5400/5500, size 5, cap 50, edge 2 below BS)
  Problem: SIZE=5 and CAP=50 are far too conservative. The taker sells
  64-81 contracts/day on 5400. At size 5, cap 50, we capture at most 50
  contracts. The taker is willing to sell 250+. We're leaving 200 contracts
  of cheap accumulation on the table.

LAYER: Deep ITM MM (4000/4500, intrinsic±1, size 30, cap 100)
  Status: Works as designed. $2,437 on 4000 day-2 10k. No change needed.

LAYER: Call-spread arb
  Status: Rarely fires (no-arb violations are rare in mids). Adds ~$0.

SUMMARY OF EDGE LEAKAGE:
  1. OTM passive bids too small (5 units, cap 50) — should be 20-30, cap 200+
  2. VEV_5000 underexploited — spread=6, zero taker flow, perfect for wide MM
  3. Passive MM on 5400/5500 at spread=1 is accidental aggressive buying
  4. BS taking edge=10 too wide for OTM — catches nothing

PART 4: WHAT SHOULD THE OPTIMAL STRATEGY BE?
========================================================================

STRIKE-SPECIFIC OPTIMAL STRATEGIES:

── VEV_5000 (spread=6, delta=0.69, zero takers) ──────────────────────

STRATEGY: Aggressive inside-spread MM.
  - Bid at best_bid+1, ask at best_ask-1 (capture ~4 ticks/round-trip)
  - Size: 30 per side
  - Position cap: ±150 (reserve for arb layers)
  - Inventory skew: when pos > 80, widen bid by 1; when pos < -80, widen ask

RISK: Delta=0.69 means ±150 position has ±103 delta. A 30-point VFE move
(typical daily range) swings PnL by ±$3,090. Must accept this inventory risk
or hedge partially via VFE position.

EXPECTED: The "invisible taker" mechanism (same as R1 ACO) should generate
fills even with zero visible taker trades. On R1, ACO had ~59 fills/2k ticks
from this mechanism. VEV_5000 should see similar. At 4 ticks/round-trip with
~30 round-trips/day, that's ~$3,600/day.

── VEV_5300 (spread=2, delta=0.29, 37-45 sells/day) ──────────────────

STRATEGY: One-sided passive accumulation + position-aware exit.
  - Post bid at best_bid+1, size 20, cap +200
  - NO ask posting (no buyer flow exists)
  - When position > 150 AND VFE is rising (VFE mid > VFE ma50), hold
  - When position > 150 AND VFE is flat/falling, post ask to shed
  - Terminal unwind: sell remaining position via ask at best_ask-1

RISK: Pure long delta (0.29 * 200 = 58 delta equivalent). On VFE down-days
this hurts. Day 0: -14 PnL. Days 1/2: +2,232 and +1,100.

EXPECTED: 37-45 fills/day at ~3.5 contracts = 130-160 accumulated. At average
cost below mid by ~0.5 ticks, plus terminal delta appreciation on up-days:
$500-2,000/day on up-days, -$500 on down-days.

── VEV_5400 (spread=1, delta=0.14, 64-81 sells/day) ──────────────────

STRATEGY: Passive bid accumulation at best_bid (NOT best_bid+1).
  - At spread=1, best_bid+1 = best_ask. Posting there = aggressive buy.
  - Instead: post bid at best_bid, size 20, cap +250
  - The taker sells AT the bid, so we get filled at our price.
  - When spread >= 2 (33-43% of ticks), post at best_bid+1 for priority.

RISK: Delta=0.14 * 250 = 35 delta. Manageable. Theta = 2.37/day on price
~15 = 16% daily decay. BUT we're buying BELOW mid, so we have a cost basis
advantage of ~0.5-1 tick vs fair value.

EXPECTED: 64-81 fills/day at ~3.5 = 224-284 contracts. Near full cap.
PnL = (terminal_mid - avg_cost) * qty. On day 2: ~+$1,737. Day 0: ~-$321.
Average across 3 days: ~$700-800/day.

── VEV_5500 (spread=1, delta=0.06, 81-94 sells/day) ──────────────────

STRATEGY: Passive bid accumulation at best_bid, size 20, cap +300.
  - Same logic as 5400 but even more one-sided.
  - Price is 5-8, theta is 1.38/day (26% daily). This is a lottery ticket.
  - Accumulate cheap, hope for VFE spike.

RISK: Delta=0.06 * 300 = 18 delta. Negligible. Max loss = 300 * 7 = $2,100
if expires worthless. But we're buying below mid, so actual max loss is lower.

EXPECTED: Fill near-full 300 contracts. PnL = small positive on up-days
(+$365 day 2), small negative on down-days (-$75 day 0). Expected ~$100/day.
Not worth optimizing heavily — the edge is tiny.

PART 5: CONCRETE CHANGES TO CURRENT CODE
========================================================================

Change 1: VEV_5000 — Add dedicated wide-spread MM layer
  Currently: only traded via intrinsic arb + BS taking + generic MM.
  The generic MM posts at bb+1/ba-1 with size 20 — fine, but size=20 is
  conservative for spread=6. Increase to 30, and add position cap ±150.
  Also: the intrinsic arb sells if price > spot + 2, which for deep ITM
  means selling at 253+2=255 when the ask is 259. This is too aggressive
  — we're hitting bids we shouldn't. For 5000, the intrinsic arb should
  use a wider sell threshold since the time value (3-7) is real edge.

Change 2: VEV_5300/5400/5500 — Scale up OTM passive bids
  Currently: SIZE=5, CAP=50, EDGE=2 below BS fair.
  Proposed: SIZE=20, CAP=200 for 5300; SIZE=20, CAP=250 for 5400;
            SIZE=20, CAP=300 for 5500. EDGE=1 (tighter).
  This captures 4x more of the sell flow that currently goes unfilled.

Change 3: VEV_5400/5500 — Fix spread=1 passive MM bug
  Currently: posts bid at bb+1 which equals the ask at spread=1. This is
  an aggressive buy, not a passive quote. Either:
  (a) Skip generic passive MM for these strikes (let OTM bid layer handle)
  (b) Only post when spread >= 2
  The current code has V_VOUCHER_MIN_SPREAD=2, which SHOULD prevent
  posting at spread=1. But checking the code: it posts at bb+1 which at
  spread=1 means posting at ba, which is allowed because bb+1 < ba is
  FALSE when spread=1 (bb+1 == ba), so the order IS placed but at the ask
  price. Need to verify this is filtered correctly.

  Actually reading the code more carefully:
    vba - vbb < V_VOUCHER_MIN_SPREAD: continue
  So spread < 2 → skip. At spread=1: 1 < 2 → skip. This IS filtered.
  The bug is in the OTM bid layer: it posts at min(bb+1, bs_cap). At
  spread=1, bb+1 = ba, then the filter "bid_px >= ba: continue" catches
  it. So the OTM bid layer also correctly skips spread=1 ticks.

  This means on 57-67% of ticks (spread=1), we post NOTHING on 5400.
  We only accumulate on the 33-43% of ticks where spread >= 2. This
  explains why the OTM layer captures so little. The fix: post at
  best_bid (not best_bid+1) when spread=1. This puts us AT the bid,
  competing with the MM bot, but the taker hits ALL bids by price
  priority per the engine rules. So posting at best_bid still gets fills.

Change 4: BS taking — Reduce edge for OTM strikes
  Currently: V_BS_EDGE=10 for all strikes.
  For 5300 (mid=47): fair-10=37 → never fires.
  Proposed: per-strike edges. For 5300: edge=4. For 5400: edge=3.
  For 5500: edge=2. These are still safe but will actually trigger.

PART 7: B&H vs MM/MT — THE DEFINITIVE ANSWER
========================================================================

                            B&H (300)      MM/Passive Bid
  VEV_5000                 -$300 to +$5k   +$2-3.6k/day (MM, consistent)
  VEV_5300                 -$500 to +$2.2k +$500-2k/day (passive, directional)
  VEV_5400                 -$300 to +$1.7k +$300-1.2k/day (passive, directional)
  VEV_5500                 -$100 to +$365  +$50-200/day (passive, tiny)

MM/passive bid DOMINATES B&H because:
  1. Lower cost basis — you buy at bid, not at ask (save 1-6 ticks/contract)
  2. Gradual accumulation — if VFE drops, you haven't committed full size yet
  3. No initial spread cost — B&H pays full ask-bid spread upfront
  4. Consistent on flat days — MM earns spread even when VFE goes nowhere

B&H only wins IF:
  - You know VFE will drift up significantly (>30 points)
  - AND you can enter at a tight spread
  - AND you hold for 5000+ ticks

For the 1k-tick website test: B&H is ALWAYS negative. MM/passive is the
only viable approach.

For 10k ticks: MM/passive wins on average. B&H wins on the best up-day
(day 2: +28 drift) but loses on down-days. Risk-adjusted, MM wins.

PART 8: WHAT WON'T WORK (CONFIRMED DEAD ENDS)
========================================================================

1. Gamma scalping: gamma ~0.001, spread cost 1-6. PnL per tick =
   0.5 * 0.001 * 9 = $0.005. Needs 200-1200 ticks to pay one spread.
   NOT VIABLE.

2. Short vol (sell OTM calls, delta-hedge): No buyer flow. You cannot
   sell into a market where the taker only sells. Your ask will never
   get lifted. NOT VIABLE.

3. Symmetric MM on 5400/5500: Spread=1 means no inside room. Even at
   spread=2, your ask never gets filled (no buyer flow). You're doing
   one-sided accumulation disguised as MM. Just accept it.

4. VFE-based timing for voucher entry: VFE has Sharpe ~0.2-0.3 at
   10-tick horizon. A 0.3 Sharpe means 53% directional accuracy.
   At delta 0.14-0.29, this gives ~0.04-0.09 ticks of edge per
   voucher entry. Spread cost is 1-2 ticks. Timing loses.
