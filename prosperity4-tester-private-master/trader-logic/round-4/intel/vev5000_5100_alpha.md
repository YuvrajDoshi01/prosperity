# VEV_5000 & VEV_5100 Alpha Hunt — R4

## 1. Why dead? Microstructure forensics (Day 3 CSV, n=10k)

| Strike | Spot dist | Extrinsic | Med spread | spread<=2 | spread>=5 | Bid/Ask vol | Trades |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5000 | -300 (deep ITM) | $3.5 mean (sd 1.5) | **6** | 0.6% | **98.0%** | 27/27 | 3 |
| 5100 | -200 (mod ITM)  | $13.2 mean (sd 3.8) | **4** | 1.4% | 26.3% | 27/27 | 3 |
| 5200 | -100 (near-ATM) | $41.3 mean (sd 7.5) | **3** | **26.0%** | 0.0% | 27/27 | 47 |

**Root cause is bot spread, not liquidity.** Quote sizes are identical (27/27). VEV_5000 trades at near-parity (extrinsic $3.5 ~= the spread itself), so MM bots quote wide (median 6, 98% >=5) to avoid arbing themselves on intrinsic; VEV_5100 sits at the boundary (med 4); VEV_5200 has enough optionality to support a 3-tick spread.

**Spread = 1.5 x edge for VEV_5000.** With BS_EDGE=10 the take threshold is never hit (`ask cheap by >=3` count = 0 across all 3 strikes); MM at vbb+1/vba-1 only fires when spread>=3 and yields 1 tick capture per round-trip — too thin to overcome adverse selection.

## 2. Activation threshold

Strike becomes tradeable when **median spread <= 3 AND spread<=2 ticks > 20%** of the day. VEV_5200 crosses both; VEV_5100 fails the second (1.4%); VEV_5000 fails both. Equivalent rule: **extrinsic > ~5x median spread**.
